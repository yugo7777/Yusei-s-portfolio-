Hero Upgrade System

tier 1

Electrician:

↓

option 1 Tier2: Solar Energy Technician

cost: 250 coins

role: install, maintain solar panels and supply sustainable energy.

attack: Solar Surge – fires a chain lightning that jumps to up to 3 enemies, each hit dealing 60% damage.

passive: damage increases by + 25% during daytime maps.

theme: highlights solar power as a clean energy source

reference:Quotefree.com.au. (2025). Solar Technicians in Australia: Careers, Skills, and Opportunities. [online] Available at: https://quotefree.com.au/blogs/solarTechnician [Accessed 22 Aug. 2025] https://quotefree.com.au/blogs/solarTechnician

option 2 Tier2: Energy system engineer

cost: 200 coins

role: designs and manages an efficient electrical system.

attack: Releases a controlled electric surge that jumps to 3 enemies, each hit dealing 50% damage and briefly paralyzing them.

passive:nearby allied tower gain +20% attack speed

theme:emphasizes how important system design and optimization

reference: Oregon State University College of Engineering. (2022). What Do Energy Systems Engineers Do? | College of Engineering. [online] Available at: https://engineering.oregonstate.edu/MIME/academics/what-do-energy-systems-engineers-do.

option 3 Tier2: commercial/ residential electrician (conventional energy source like coal or gas)

cost: 100 coins

role: supply electricity but continues to rely on conventional energy sources such as coal and gas.

attack: lightning strikes enemies around the tower. deals heavy damage to all enemies hit and temporarily paralysed them

passive:

  • Tower continuously loses 2% of max HP every 5 seconds due to electrical inefficiency (HP cannot drop below 20% from this effect)

or

  • After each surge, nearby allied towers lose 100 point for 6 seconds

theme:conventional energy is powerful in the short term, but lacks sustainability.

reference: Egt.net.au. (2025). The Different Types of Electricians in Australia. [online] Available at: https://egt.net.au/news-insights/the-different-types-of-electricians-in-australia.

↓

option 1 Tier2: Water engineers

cost:250

role: specializes in greywater recycling and rainwater collection system to conserve resources.

attack: creates a puddle zone radius 1 panel that slows enemies by 30% for 6 seconds.

passive: enemies standing in the puddle take +25% extra damage from electrician towers.

theme: Highlights water conservation and sustainable reuse of resources.

reference: Indeed Career Guide. (2023). What Does a Water Engineer Do? (And How to Become One). [online] Available at: https://au.indeed.com/career-advice/finding-a-job/what-does-water-engineer-do.

option 2 Tier2: pipe system engineer

cost:200

role: installs and maintains advanced plumbing and pressure systems that improve efficiency in water distribution.

attack: fires a concentrated water blast, piercing through up to 3 enemies in a line.

passive: nearby allied towers gain +10% attack speed

theme: highlights efficiency and modern plumbing tech that is feasible to reduce wast

reference: https://www.facebook.com/sanalbn. (2023, May 15). Piping Engineer Roles and Responsibilities - ASTS GLOBAL. ASTS GLOBAL. https://astsglobal.com/blog/responsibilities-and-duties-of-a-piping-engineer/

option 3 Tier2: PVC Pipe Installer or bypass drain plumber

cost:100

role: installs plumbing system using cheap PVC piping. although cost effective and quick set up, pvc is likely to produce long term environmental harm

attack: fires a water blast, dealing damage over time to all enemies hit.

passive: after each attack, nearby allied towers could lose 3% health points(representing having sustainability issues).

theme: PVC pipe can be effective in the short term but create serious sustainability issue in the long term

reference:PVC Below Ground Installation - Vinidex Pty Ltd. (2024, October 25). Vinidex Pty Ltd. https://www.vinidex.com.au/resources/technical-resources/pvc-below-ground-installation/

Eco-Rescue: Build, Save, and Thrive

A fast-paced eco-strategy game where players design sustainable neighborhoods to protect the environment and inspire future builders.

Game concept overview

'Eco-Rescue: build, save and survive! is a strategic simulation game designed for children aged 10-12. Players face environmental challenges such as pollution, flooding and resource scarcity while building eco-friendly homes and public infrastructure. The game is structured to make sustainability a fun learning experience and a natural introduction to careers in the construction industry.

  • infrastructure development: restore basic community functions by locating eco-friendly housing and public facilities to support residents' livelihoods.
  • environmental improvement: forest bathing areas (trees and flower beds) to revive the ecosystem.
  • Resource management: develop towns efficiently by utilising renewable energy and recycled materials, taking into account budgetary and material constraints.
  • Crisis response: whenever environmental hazards such as floods or pollution clouds occur, rapid response measures are implemented to ensure the safety of the population.
  • Through these activities, players naturally learn the principles of sustainability and at the same time develop an interest in the construction industry professions.

Game structure and progression flow

The game consists of a total of five stages. Each stage has different environmental conditions.

Each stage has its own set of achievement requirements, such as “sustainability score”, “inhabitant happiness”, “energy efficiency”, etc.

As players complete each stage, new building materials and hero abilities are unlocked, expanding the scope of their strategies.

The final stage tests whether the entire town built by the player can withstand the effects of climate change.

Gameplay phases

1.The Build Phase

  • At the beginning of each round, players receive a limited budget (e.g. $5000000).
  • They choose from a selection of eco-friendly or non-eco-friendly materials to build their houses and facilities.
  • The choice of materials and buildings has a direct impact on pollution, energy efficiency and community well-being.

Examples of construction options:

Solar roofs: generate renewable energy and reduce electricity costs.

Green roofs: increase biodiversity and reduce the urban heat island effect.

Permeable pavements: reduce flooding and stormwater run-off.

Plastic cladding: inexpensive, but causes health problems.

Asbestos insulation: very inexpensive, but significantly reduces health and sustainability.

2. eco-hero phase

Players can hire professional characters to support sustainable development:

Eco-Electrician: enhances solar technology and reduces electronics-related costs.

Green Plumber: prevents water pollution and enhances eco-facilities.

Construction hero: speeds up the installation of eco-building materials.

These characters act as role models for real-life construction jobs.

3. challenge phase

At the end of each round, an environmental challenge occurs:

Flooding

Pollution clouds

Power outages

Players utilise their previous choices and hero deployments to defend the region. Scores are assessed according to ecological recovery, well-being of the local population and health restoration.

Educational value

Learn about the long-term benefits of sustainable development.

Develop logical thinking and strategic skills through resource management.

Learn about construction-related careers in a fun, age-appropriate way for children.

Compatibility with this project.

The concept of this game fits with the objectives of the CSQ:

  • Raise awareness of sustainability.
  • Positive and engaging introduction to the construction industry profession.
  • Provides a fun, educational and manipulative gaming experience for children.
